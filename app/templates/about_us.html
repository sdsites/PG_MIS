ABOUT PG MIS

Keeping Producer Group work in one place.

Managing a Producer Group involves more than keeping a member list. There are meetings to record, funds to track, business activities to update and reports to prepare. PG MIS brings this information together, making the day-to-day work easier to manage and follow up.

Designed around the work of Producer Groups and field teams in Tripura.

How PG MIS works

  1. 01Record

    Enter group information and regular activity details in the right section.

  2. 02Review

    Records that require approval go to the assigned person for checking.

  3. 03Follow up

    Check pending work, view updates and use reports to see progress.

WHAT THE SYSTEM COVERS

The work you do, organised

Different sections for the records your team works with every day.

Group records

PG & member details

Keep Producer Group profiles, member information and SHG links together. Update details as groups and memberships change.

Meetings

Meetings & registers

Record meeting dates, attendance, discussions and decisions. Keep routine registers available when they are needed.

Finance

Funds, loans & grants

Enter cash transactions, loans and grant information in their respective registers, with records that can be checked later.

Business

Stock & business

Keep track of stock, production and business activities. Add monthly updates so the group’s work is properly recorded.

Approvals

Review & validation

Submit records for review where required. Assigned users can check the information and take the next action.

Reporting

Dashboards & reports

See available summaries, check the status of records and follow progress across groups, based on your access.

Who uses PG MIS?

PG data-entry users, community cadres, CLF teams and authorised block, district and state staff. Each person sees the sections needed for their assigned work.
