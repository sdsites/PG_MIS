About PG MIS

A simpler way to manage Producer Group records.

PG MIS brings everyday Producer Group information into one place. From member details and meeting records to finance, business activities and reports, it helps field teams keep records organised and makes follow-up easier.

Built around the working needs of Producer Groups in Tripura.

How the system works

  1. 01
    Record

    Enter group, member and activity details in the relevant registers.

  2. 02
    Review

    Send records that need approval to the appropriate reviewer.

  3. 03
    Monitor

    Use dashboards and reports to follow progress and check records.

What you can do

Day-to-day work, in one system

Each section is organised around the records and activities managed by project teams.

01 / GROUP RECORDS

PG & member details

Maintain group profiles, member information and SHG links.

02 / MEETINGS

Meetings & registers

Keep track of meetings, minutes and routine records.

03 / FINANCE

Funds, loans & grants

Record cash movement, member loans and grant details.

04 / BUSINESS

Stock & business

Update stock, production and monthly business activities.

05 / APPROVALS

Review & validation

Submit and review information through assigned approval steps.

06 / REPORTING

Dashboards & reports

See progress across groups and locations, based on access rights.

Made for the people managing the work

PG data-entry users, community cadres, CLF teams and authorised block, district and state staff use PG MIS according to their assigned responsibilities.
